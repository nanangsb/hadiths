Bengali

English

It was narrated from Jabir bin ‘Abdullah that the Messenger of Allah (ﷺ) said:“Do not leave dinner, even if it is only a handful of dates, because abandoning it makes one weak.”

Turkish

Câbir bin Abdillah (r.a.)'dan rivayet edildiğine göre; Resulullah (Sallallahu Aleyhi ve Sellem) şöyle buyurdu, demiştir: Bir avuç kuru hurma ile de olsa akşam yemeğini terketmeyiniz.
