English

Sa'id ibn Amr al-Qurashi quoting his father said:Ibn Umar (once) saw some fellow travellers of the Yemen. They had their saddles (on camels) of leather. He said: If anyone likes to see the fellow travellers most resembling to the Companions of the Messenger of Allah (ﷺ), he should see them

French

Sa'id ibn Amr al-Qurashi, citant son père, a dit : Ibn Umar a vu un jour des voyageurs du Yémen. Ils avaient des selles en cuir sur leurs chameaux. Il a dit : « Si quelqu’un veut voir les voyageurs qui ressemblent le plus aux Compagnons du Messager d’Allah ﷺ, qu’il les regarde. »

Russian
